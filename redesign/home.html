Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

Most standard business websites are completed in 2 to 3 weeks. E-commerce platforms and complex custom portals typically require 3 to 5 weeks depending on catalogue size.

Can I accept local Ethiopian payments?

Yes. We integrate local payment options such as Telebirr and CBE Birr, as well as international processors like Stripe and PayPal for overseas clients.

Do I own the website after launch?

Absolutely. You own 100% of the code, domain, database, and assets. We do not lock you into expensive mandatory monthly maintenance contracts.
